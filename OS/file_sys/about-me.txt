Hello, my name is Sharvesh - professinally a product designer , guided by the kid i still am at heart.

For as long as I can remember, I’ve been obsessed with the magic inside a screen. Long before I knew what “UI/UX” meant, I was the neighborhood kid hunting down Windows Vista/7 transformation packs to reskin my Windows XP, just to get closer to that shiny Aero theme. That was my earliest taste of design—the simple joy of bending a digital space to my imagination.

I still vividly remember the leap from my Samsung Metro to my first Android device: the fluid animations, the polished visual language, the intuitive interactions. I could feel every shift, and that awareness was the designer in me waking up.

Though I pursued Computer Science, Google’s product videos—especially those unforgettable Secret Santa gems—pushed me to teach myself After Effects, chasing the magic I still admire. That’s when I realized: I love technology for how it works, but even more for how it’s experienced.

Over time, the bridge between my technical background and my love for design built itself naturally, leading me into Product Design—and I couldn’t ask for anything better. I’m still grateful that the kid who just wanted to play with cool interfaces is the one running the show in my career today.
